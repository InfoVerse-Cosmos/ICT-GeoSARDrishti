# Landing page assets and references

- Rotating globe texture: NASA Earth Observatory **Blue Marble: Next Generation**, January 2004 global base map. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/
  Texture: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-base/january/world.200401.3x5400x2700.jpg
  The globe is a decorative visualization, not live satellite data. Lighting and atmosphere are illustrative.

- Earth photograph: **Blue Marble, Eastern Hemisphere**, March 30, 2014 composite. NASA Earth Observatory image by Robert Simmon, using Suomi NPP VIIRS imagery from NOAA’s Environmental Visualization Laboratory.
  Source: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/blue-marble-eastern-hemisphere/
  Image: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/dynamicimage/assets/science/psd/solar/2023/09/b/bluemarble_2014089_lrg.jpg?fit=clip&w=1600
  The photograph illustrates Earth and is not presented as a NISAR or SAR observation.
- Radar examples and team artwork: reproduced from the user-provided `geosar-intelligence/frontend/public` reference project. Radar examples are labelled OPERA RTC-S1 / Sentinel-1, July 2022, distinct from this application’s NISAR packs.
- Fonts: Inter, Public Sans and DM Mono, distributed under the SIL Open Font License. License files are included in `/fonts/`.
  Font sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/inter , https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/publicsans , https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmmono
- Visual reference: https://www.nasa.gov/
- Typography reference: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/brand-guidelines/
